ENGR 321 Project 2 - Drone Delivery Simulator

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder first (right-click > Extract All on Windows, or double-click on Mac).
2. Open Google Chrome.
3. Drag drone_sim.html into the Chrome window (or right-click it > Open with > Google Chrome).
   - Do not open it from inside the email or a preview window. It will not run there.
   - Use a laptop or desktop computer, not a phone or tablet.
4. The setup screen should appear. Check that the "Graphics" line says OK.

IF THE SCREEN IS BLACK OR GRAPHICS SAYS NOT AVAILABLE
Chrome > Settings > System > turn on "Use graphics acceleration when available", then fully quit and reopen Chrome.

FILES
- drone_sim.html: the simulator (works offline, nothing to install)
- Project2_Analysis_Workbook.xlsx: blank analysis workbook (the simulator's "Export results workbook" button makes a filled copy)
